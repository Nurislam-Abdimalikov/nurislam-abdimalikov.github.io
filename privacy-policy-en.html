In short

We do not collect any data about you or your child ourselves — no sign-up, names, e-mails or analytics. Progress is stored only on the device. The game shows Google AdMob ads configured as child-directed: non-personalized and without the advertising ID. The Android version has no paid purchases. The iPhone and iPad version offers App Store purchases behind a parental gate; we never see payment details.

What is stored on the device

  • progress: cleared levels, stars, coins, unlocked and upgraded heroes, daily gifts;
  • settings: sound, music, vibration, screen shake, language;
  • on iPhone and iPad — App Store purchase records: the end date of the "No Ads for 30 Days" pass, purchase IDs and dates (so nothing is credited twice and the pass can be restored).

This data is never sent to us — the game has no servers of its own. You can delete it with the "Reset progress" button in the game settings or by deleting the game.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads through Google AdMob (Google). There are two kinds: optional reward videos the player chooses to watch (coins, a second chance, a double reward) and occasionally a short ad between levels (never during a battle); on iPhone and iPad it can be removed for 30 days with the "No Ads for 30 Days" pass.

All ad requests are marked as child-directed (COPPA, "under the age of consent"), limited to "General audiences" (G) content and non-personalized ads only; the device advertising ID (Android Advertising ID / IDFA) is not used — on Android its permission is removed from the app, on iPhone and iPad the game never shows the tracking prompt. Users in Europe and the UK may see Google's consent form.

To serve ads, measure delivery and prevent fraud, Google may automatically receive technical information: IP address (used to estimate approximate location such as country or city), device model and OS version, language, technical app identifiers (not advertising IDs), ad views and taps, crash and performance data. Google processes it under its own policies: Google Privacy Policy, How Google uses information from partner apps.

Purchases

Android

No paid purchases — every hero and upgrade is unlocked with coins earned in the game.

iPhone and iPad

You can buy coin packs and the "No Ads for 30 Days" pass. The pass never renews automatically: you pay once, and after 30 days ads between levels come back. All heroes and upgrades can also be unlocked for free with coins earned in battles.

Before any purchase the game shows a parental gate — a simple task for a grown-up. Payment is handled entirely by the App Store (Apple): we never see or store card details, name, e-mail or Apple ID. The game only receives a purchase confirmation from the App Store (which product, purchase ID and date) to credit coins, count the pass days and bring back the remaining days with "Restore Purchases". This information stays on the device. Refunds follow Apple's rules: reportaproblem.apple.com.

What the game does not do

  • does not ask for a name, e-mail, phone number, photos, contacts or precise location;
  • does not use analytics or tracking services;
  • has no chats, no communication with other players and no social media links;
  • does not sell or share data with anyone, except for the ad serving described above.

Permissions

Internet — for ads (and purchases on iPhone/iPad); the game works offline too. Vibration — feedback on hits, can be turned off in the settings. The game has no access to the camera, microphone, photos, files, contacts or location and never shows the tracking prompt.

Children

The game is made for kids and families. We follow COPPA, GDPR (including its rules for children) and the Google Play Families Policy and the App Store Review Guidelines: no personal data of children is collected, ads are child-directed and non-personalized, purchases (on iPhone and iPad) are only possible behind a parental gate. If you believe a child's data has reached us by mistake, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date.
